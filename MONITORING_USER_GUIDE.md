# MONITORING SYSTEM - USER GUIDE

Welcome to the Phone Retailer Management System's comprehensive Monitoring and Support module. This guide explains how users (Shop Admins and Staff) can interact with the support system and how SuperAdmins can monitor platform health.

---

## 🛠 For Shop Admins & Staff (Users)

### 1. Reporting an Issue
You can report issues directly from the context of what you are doing. Look for the **"Report"** or **"Dispute"** buttons in the following locations:
- **Product Details**: Flag incorrect listings or suspicious items.
- **Order Details**: Dispute an order, report a payment issue, or signal a shipping problem.
- **Seller Profiles**: Report suspicious or fraudulent behavior by other marketplace participants.

### 2. The Report Wizard
Our 4-step wizard makes reporting easy:
1. **Type**: Select what the issue is (Dispute, Technical, Fraud, etc.).
2. **Details**: Provide a subject and detailed description. The system automatically attaches relevant IDs (Order #, Listing ID).
3. **Review**: Verify your report before sending.
4. **Ticket Number**: Receive a unique tracking number (e.g., `TKT-2026-XXXX`).

### 3. Tracking Your Tickets
Go to **Support > My Tickets** to:
- See a list of all your active and past tickets.
- View the real-time status (Open, In Progress, Awaiting Response, Resolved).
- Read the full conversation thread with support staff.
- Send follow-up messages or attachments.

---

## 👁 For SuperAdmins

### 1. The SuperAdmin Dashboard
The Overview Dashboard provides a "bird's eye view" of the entire platform:
- **System Health**: Uptime metrics and API latency.
- **User Health**: Active users (DAU/MAU) and growth trends.
- **Error Health**: Error rates and critical diagnostic alerts.
- **Business Health**: Revenue trends and transaction volume.

### 2. Managing the Support Queue
Navigate to **Management > Support Queue** to handle incoming requests:
- **Unified List**: Filter by priority, status, or type.
- **Contextual Detail**: Click any ticket to see the full conversation and the related Order/Listing.
- **Admin Actions**: Respond to users, change status, or assign tickets to other staff members.

---

## 🔐 Security & Privacy
- **Data Isolation**: Users only see tickets they created.
- **Admin Privacy**: Internal notes and status history are only visible to SuperAdmins.
- **Encryption**: All communication is secured via HTTPS/SSL.
