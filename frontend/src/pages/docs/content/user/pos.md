# Point of Sale (POS)

The POS module is the heart of your daily operations, designed for fast and efficient checkout.

## Overview

The POS screen allows you to adds products to the cart, manage customer details, and process payments.

## How to Process a Sale

1.  **Search for Products**: Use the search bar to find products by name or SKU. You can also browse the product grid.
2.  **Add to Cart**: Click on a product to add it to the cart.
    *   If a product has variants (e.g., color, storage), a popup will appear for you to select the specific option.
3.  **Adjust Cart**:
    *   **Quantity**: Use the `+` and `-` buttons to change quantity.
    *   **Remove**: Click the trash icon to remove an item.
4.  **Customer Details (Optional)**:
    *   Walk-in customers are selected by default.
    *   To link a sale to a specific customer, use the "Select Customer" dropdown.
5.  **Checkout**:
    *   Review the total amount.
    *   Select the payment method (Cash, Card, Transfer).
    *   Click **Complete Sale**.

## Receipt Generation

After a successful sale, a receipt is automatically generated. You can:
*   Print the receipt immediately.
*   Email the receipt to the customer (if customer email is provided).
*   Start a new sale.
