# User Profile

Manage your account settings and preferences.

## Accessing Profile

Click on your name or avatar in the top right corner and select **Profile** (or navigate to **User Profile** from the sidebar).

## Available Settings

*   **Personal Information**: View your name and email address.
*   **Change Password**:
    1.  Enter your current password.
    2.  Enter your new password.
    3.  Confirm the new password.
    4.  Click **Update Password**.

> **Security Tip**: Choose a strong password and change it regularly to keep your account secure.
