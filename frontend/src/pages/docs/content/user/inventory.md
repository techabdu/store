# Inventory Lookup

The Inventory pages allows staff members to check stock levels and view product details without the ability to modify critical data.

## Checking Stock

1.  Navigate to the **Inventory** page from the sidebar.
2.  **Search**: Use the search bar to find a specific product.
3.  **Filter**: Use filters to narrow down by category or brand.

## Product Details

The inventory list shows:
*   **Product Name**: Full name of the device or accessory.
*   **SKU**: Unique Stock Keeping Unit identifier.
*   **Stock Level**: Current quantity on hand.
    *   <span style="color: red">Low Stock</span>: Indicates items running low.
    *   <span style="color: green">In Stock</span>: Healthy stock levels.
*   **Price**: Selling price of the item.

> **Note**: As a standard user, you cannot change prices or stock levels manually. If you notice a discrepancy, please report it to an Admin.
