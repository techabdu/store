# Sales History

View your personal sales records and reprint receipts.

## Viewing Your Sales

1.  Navigate to **Sales History**.
2.  The list displays all sales processed by your account.
3.  **Date Range**: content defaults to today's sales. Use the date picker to view past records.

## Sales Details

Each record shows:
*   **Receipt ID**: Unique identifier for the transaction.
*   **Date & Time**: When the sale occurred.
*   **Customer**: Name of the customer (or "Walk-in").
*   **Total Amount**: Final value of the sale.
*   **Items**: Summary of items sold.

## Actions

*   **View Receipt**: Click the eye icon or the row to view the detailed receipt.
*   **Print**: From the detail view, you can reprint the receipt if needed.
