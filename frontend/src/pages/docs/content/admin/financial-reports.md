# Financial Reports

Understand your profitability with financial insights.

## Profit & Loss

Navigate to **Report** > **Financial** to view your Profit & Loss statement.

*   **Revenue**: Total income from sales.
*   **COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**: Total cost of the items sold (based on the Cost Price entered in inventory).
*   **Operating Expenses**: Total from the Expenses module.
*   **Net Profit**: Revenue - COGS - Expenses.

## Cash Flow

Tracks the movement of money in and out of your business, categorized by payment methods. Useful for reconciling the cash drawer at the end of the day.
