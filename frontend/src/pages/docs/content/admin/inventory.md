# Inventory Management (Admin)

As an Admin, you have full control over the shop's inventory. You can add new products, update existing ones, and remove obsolete items.

## Adding a New Product

1.  Navigate to **Inventory**.
2.  Click the **Add Product** button (usually at the top right).
3.  Fill in the product details:
    *   **Name**: Full product name.
    *   **SKU**: Unique identifier.
    *   **Category**: Grouping for the product.
    *   **Brand**: Product brand.
    *   **Price**: Selling price.
    *   **Cost Price**: (Optional) For profit calculation.
    *   **Quantity**: Initial stock level.
4.  **Variants**: If the product has options (e.g., Color, Storage), add them in the variants section.
5.  Click **Save Product**.

## Editing a Product

1.  Find the product in the list.
2.  Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon.
3.  Update the necessary fields.
4.  Click **Update**.

## Deleting a Product

1.  Find the product.
2.  Click the **Delete** (trash) icon.
3.  Confirm the deletion in the popup.

> **Warning**: Deleting a product removes it permanently. Historical sales data for this product will remain, but it will no longer be available for new sales.
