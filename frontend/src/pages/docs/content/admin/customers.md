# Customer Management

Build relationships with your clients by managing their profiles.

## Customer Database

Navigate to **Customers** to see a list of all registered clients.

*   **Search**: Find customers by name or phone number.
*   **Purchase History**: Click on a customer to view their past purchases and total lifetime value.

## Adding a Customer

You can add customers directly from the POS or the Customer Management page.
*   Required: Name.
*   Recommended: Phone Number, Email (for digital receipts).

## Editing Customers

Update contact information or add notes to a customer profile at any time.
