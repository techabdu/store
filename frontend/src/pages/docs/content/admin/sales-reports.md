# Sales Reports

Monitor your shop's performance with detailed sales history and reports.

## Sales History

The **Sales History** page shows a chronological list of all transactions across the shop.

*   **Filters**: Filter by date range, specific cashier (user), or payment method.
*   **Export**: Option to export data to CSV/Excel for external analysis.

## Daily Sales Report

In the **Report** section, you can generate a **Daily Sales Report**.
*   Shows total sales for the selected day.
*   Breakdown by payment method (Cash, Card, Transfer).
*   Summary of items sold.

## Reversing a Sale

If a refund is needed:
1.  Locate the transaction in Sales History.
2.  Open the receipt details.
3.  (If feature enabled) Click **Refund/Reverse**.
4.  The system will adjust stock levels and financial records accordingly.
