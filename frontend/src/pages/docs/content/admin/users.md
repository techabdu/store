# User Management

Control who has access to your shop's system.

## Roles

*   **Admin**: Full access to all features (Inventory, Reports, Settings).
*   **User**: Restricted access (POS, Sales History, Personal Profile).

## Adding a Staff Member

1.  Navigate to **Users** (or User Management).
2.  Click **Add User**.
3.  Enter details: Name, Email, Role (User), Password.
4.  Click **Create**.

## Managing Access

*   **Deactivate**: If a staff member leaves, you can deactivate their account to prevent login without deleting their sales history.
*   **Reset Password**: Admins can reset passwords for staff members who are locked out.
