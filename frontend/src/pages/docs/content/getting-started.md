# Getting Started with PRHub

Welcome to PRHub! This guide will help you set up your account and get your shop running in minutes.

## 1. Registration

To start using PRHub, you need to create a shop account.

1.  Navigate to the **Register** page.
2.  Fill in your **Owner Information** (Name, Email, Password).
3.  Enter your **Shop Information** (Shop Name, Currency, Address).
4.  Click **Create Account**.

> **Note**: The email address you provide will be the primary contact for your shop and will be used for important notifications.

## 2. Logging In

Once registered, you can log in to your dashboard:

1.  Go to the **Login** page.
2.  Enter your registered email and password.
3.  Click **Sign In**.

If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link to initiate a reset via email.

## 3. Initial Setup Checklist

After your first login as an Admin, we recommend completing these steps:

*   **Add Inventory**: Go to the Inventory section and start adding your products.
*   **Create User Accounts**: If you have staff, create accounts for them under User Management so they can access the POS.
*   **Review Settings**: Check the Admin Settings to ensure your shop details are correct.

You are now ready to start selling!
